# Pest Control and Dengue Prevention
Updated: 2026-03

Town Councils work closely with the National Environment Agency (NEA) to manage vectors, eradicate pests, and prevent dengue fever transmission across housing estates.

Routine Town Council Preventive Measures:
- Anti-Mosquito Oiling: Regular application of Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis (BTI) biological larvicide and eco-friendly oil in public perimeter drains and sumps to kill mosquito larvae.
- Thermal Fogging and Misting: Conducted weekly in common landscaped areas, bin chutes, and shrubbery to eliminate adult Aedes mosquitoes, especially in active dengue clusters.
- Rodent and Cockroach Control: Routine placement of tamper-resistant bait stations and scheduled bin chute fogging to prevent vermin infestation.

Resident Responsibilities (Mozzie Wipeout):
- Change water in flower vases and plant drip trays every other day.
- Scrub the inside surfaces of flower vases and containers to remove mosquito eggs.
- Invert all water storage pails and buckets when not in use.
- Ensure air-con condensation drainage trays do not retain stagnant water.
- Keep roof gutters clear of fallen leaves and place BTI insecticide tablets monthly.

Reporting Mosquito Breeding Sites:
- Residents who notice stagnant water pooling in ground depressions, choked public perimeter drains, or unusual pest activity should report the location immediately through EstateKaki or the Town Council hotline for prompt site inspection.
