# Bird Feeding, Wildlife, and Pet Guidelines
Updated: 2026-03

Under Singapore laws and Town Council by-laws, public feeding of wildlife and stray birds is strictly prohibited to safeguard public hygiene and prevent disease transmission.

Prohibition on Feeding Pigeons, Crows, and Mynas:
- Under the Wildlife Act, feeding of pigeons, crows, or any wild animals is an offence punishable by hefty fines:
  - First offence: Up to $5,000 fine.
  - Repeat offences: Up to $10,000 fine.
- Leftover bird food (rice, bread crumbs) attracts pest rats, increases pigeon populations, and causes bird droppings that dirty common corridors, laundry, and air-con ledges.

Community Cats and Trap-Neuter-Rehome-Manage (TNRM):
- Responsible feeding of community cats is permitted if caregivers follow NParks/AVS guidelines:
  - Feed cats only in designated clean areas (away from void deck seating and lift lobbies).
  - Clean up all food containers, cans, and leftover food within two (2) hours of feeding.
  - Never leave food scattered on the floor.

Dog Regulations in HDB:
- HDB flat owners are permitted to keep one (1) approved dog from the scheduled list of small breeds, or an adopted medium-sized local mixed-breed under Project ADORE (Animal Development and Ownership Rehoming Scheme).
- Dogs must be leashed in common property (corridors, lifts, void decks, parks) at all times.
- Owners must immediately pick up and properly dispose of their pet's droppings.
