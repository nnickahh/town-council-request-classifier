# Emergency Lift Breakdowns and EMSU Services
Updated: 2026-03

The Essential Maintenance Service Unit (EMSU) operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year to handle urgent municipal emergencies in HDB estates.

What EMSU Handles:
- Passenger lift rescue and lift breakdowns (passengers trapped in lift car).
- Sudden water supply failure or burst main water pipes in common areas.
- Severe electrical power trips affecting common corridors, lift lobbies, or streetlights.
- Serious ceiling leaks in common areas or sewage pipe chokes causing backflow.
- Fallen tree branches blocking roadways or posing immediate safety danger.

Lift Trapped Emergency Procedures:
- If trapped inside an HDB lift:
  1. Stay calm. The lift car is well-ventilated and supported by multiple steel hoist cables.
  2. Press and hold the yellow or orange 'Emergency Call' / 'Alarm' button inside the lift car for 3 to 5 seconds to connect directly to the 24-hour EMSU operator.
  3. Do NOT attempt to force open the lift doors or climb out through the roof.
  4. EMSU rescue technicians are dispatched with a target arrival time within 15 to 20 minutes.

Non-Emergency Maintenance:
- Minor issues (such as a single flickering fluorescent bulb, squeaky lift door, or sweeping requests) should be submitted through regular Town Council channels or the EstateKaki bot during normal operating hours rather than EMSU hotline.
