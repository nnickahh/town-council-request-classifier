# Common Corridor and Fire Safety Guidelines
Updated: 2026-03

Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) fire safety guidelines and Town Council by-laws strictly regulate the use of common property and corridors in all HDB residential estates.

The 1.2-Metre Clear Passage Rule:
- A minimum clear escape passage of at least 1.2 metres (4 feet) must be maintained along common corridors and staircases at all times.
- This unobstructed space is vital for rapid emergency evacuation and allows paramedics carrying stretcher trolleys and firefighters in full protective gear to pass unhindered.

Prohibited Items in Common Areas:
- Accumulation of combustible items such as cardboard boxes, old newspapers, wooden pallets, and discarded mattresses.
- Placing bulky items, clothes drying racks, or furniture that narrows the corridor below the required 1.2-metre width.
- Obstruction of fire hose reels, dry risers, fire alarm call points, and stairwell doors.
- Parking personal mobility devices (PMDs), e-bikes, or motorized wheelchairs across escape paths.

Potted Plants and Shoe Racks:
- Shoe racks and potted plants are permitted provided they are neatly placed against the outer wall of the resident's flat and do not reduce the remaining corridor width below 1.2 metres.
- Residents must place plant drip trays beneath pots to prevent water accumulation that causes mosquito breeding.

Enforcement:
- Town Councils conduct regular block inspections. Residents with clutter will be served written advisory notices to remove items within a grace period. Unclaimed items posing fire hazards will be cleared and disposed of by council staff.
