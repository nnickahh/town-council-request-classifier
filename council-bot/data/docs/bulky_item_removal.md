# Bulky Item Removal Service
Updated: 2026-03

Town Councils in Singapore provide free bulky item removal service for HDB residents to help keep common corridors and void decks clutter-free and fire-safe.

Key Rules and Quota:
- Each HDB household is entitled to a free removal of up to three (3) movable bulky items per calendar month.
- Additional bulky items beyond the three free items will be subject to nominal disposal charges (typically $10 to $30 per item depending on size).

Items Eligible for Free Removal:
- Household furniture (sofas, beds, wardrobes, mattresses, tables, chairs).
- Large domestic electrical appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, televisions).

Items Excluded (Residents Must Arrange Own Disposal):
- Renovation debris, building materials, broken floor tiles, and hacking waste.
- Commercial or business trade waste.
- Built-in fixtures, large fixed kitchen cabinets, and sanitary ware (toilet bowls, bathtubs).
- Extremely heavy items exceeding safe manual lifting limits (such as upright pianos or heavy iron safes).

How to Book:
- Residents should contact their respective Town Council office or submit an online booking through the Town Council website or mobile app at least three (3) to five (5) working days in advance.
- On the confirmed collection date, place items neatly outside the flat or at designated collection points by 9:00 AM without blocking common corridors or lift lobbies.
