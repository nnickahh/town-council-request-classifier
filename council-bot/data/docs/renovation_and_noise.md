# Renovation Regulations and Estate Quiet Hours
Updated: 2026-03

To maintain peaceful residential living in Singapore HDB estates, renovation works, drilling, and noise levels are regulated by Town Councils and HDB guidelines.

Permitted Renovation Hours:
- General Renovation Works: Mondays to Saturdays between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
- Heavy / Noisy Works (Hacking walls, drilling concrete, tile cutting): Permitted only on Mondays to Fridays between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Noisy demolition works must not exceed three (3) consecutive days.
- Sundays and Public Holidays: STRICTLY NO noisy renovation or demolition works allowed at any time.

Estate Quiet Hours:
- Community quiet hours are observed every night from 10:30 PM to 7:00 AM.
- During quiet hours, residents are expected to keep volume low, avoid loud music or television, refrain from dragging heavy furniture, and keep social conversations down.

Renovation Notices:
- Contractors must obtain an official HDB renovation permit and post notice outside the flat and on the block notice board at least three (3) days before starting works to alert neighbours.

Handling Noise Complaints and Disputes:
- Minor issues should first be addressed neighbourly with mutual communication.
- Chronic noise disturbances or unauthorized after-hours renovation can be reported to the Town Council or HDB Branch Office for investigation. Serious uncooperative neighbour disputes may be referred to the Community Mediation Centre (CMC).
