# Void Deck and Multi-Purpose Hall Bookings
Updated: 2026-03

Town Councils manage the booking of common spaces in HDB estates, including void decks, linkway plazas, pavilion halls, and multi-purpose courts for community, family, and religious occasions.

Permitted Event Types:
- Funeral wakes and memorial services.
- Wedding receptions and solemnization ceremonies.
- Religious functions (e.g., Seventh Month celebrations, festive gatherings).
- Resident community events, birthday celebrations, and cultural activities.

Booking Procedures and Lead Time:
- Funeral Wakes: Can be booked immediately upon production of the Death Certificate and Death Registration. Walk-in bookings are accommodated on an urgent priority basis.
- Weddings & Social Events: Should be booked at least two (2) to four (4) weeks in advance through the Town Council counter or online portal. Spaces are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

Fees, Deposits, and Utilities:
- Daily space rental fees range from $50 to $150 per day depending on the facility size and type.
- Refundable security deposit (typically $200 to $500) is required and returned after the event if the premises are clean and free of damage.
- Utilities (water tap and power supply socket connections) are metered or charged at flat daily rates.

Regulations for Event Organisers:
- Noisy activities and amplified sound must cease strictly by 10:30 PM out of consideration for surrounding residents.
- Organizers must ensure proper waste disposal and wash the floor area after event conclusion.
