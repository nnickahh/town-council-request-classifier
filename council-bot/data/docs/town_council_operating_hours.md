# Town Council Branch Offices and Operating Hours
Updated: 2026-03

Town Councils in Singapore maintain main and branch offices situated across their respective constituencies to serve residents.

Standard Counter Operating Hours:
- Mondays to Fridays: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Saturdays: 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- Sundays and Public Holidays: Closed

Counter Services Offered:
- Payment of Service & Conservancy Charges (S&CC).
- Application and payment for void deck and pavilion facility bookings.
- In-person booking for bulky item removal service.
- Verification and processing of season parking or permit documents.
- Lodging feedback or discussing estate improvement suggestions with resident service officers.

24/7 Digital Self-Service:
- Online payments, bulky removal booking, and maintenance reports can be lodged 24 hours a day via the EstateKaki Telegram bot or the Town Council official website.

Emergency Contact:
- For emergency lift breakdown, power failure in common areas, or fallen trees after operating hours, call the 24-hour EMSU (Essential Maintenance Service Unit) hotline.
