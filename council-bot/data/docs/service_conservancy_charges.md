# Service & Conservancy Charges (S&CC)
Updated: 2026-03

Service and Conservancy Charges (S&CC) are monthly fees paid by HDB flat owners and commercial tenants to their Town Council for the upkeep, cleaning, maintenance, and improvement of common property in public housing estates.

What S&CC Funds and Covers:
- Daily cleaning and sweeping of common corridors, void decks, and open public spaces.
- Regular maintenance and safety servicing of passenger lifts.
- Landscaping, grass cutting, and tree pruning.
- Pest control, fogging, and anti-mosquito oiling in drains.
- Scheduled cyclical works including block repainting, re-roofing, and pump room servicing.
- Common area electrical and water utilities (void deck lighting, lift power, corridor lights).

Payment Due Date:
- S&CC is due on the first day of every month, with payment accepted throughout the month. Late payment penalties may apply if outstanding balances are not settled.

Payment Methods:
- Interbank GIRO (automatic monthly deduction, highly recommended).
- Digital payment apps: AXS e-Station, AXS m-Station, SAM kiosks, and Town Council mobile portals.
- Internet Banking / PayNow using the Town Council S&CC account reference number.
- In-person at any Town Council branch counter during operating hours.

Government S&CC Rebates:
- Eligible Singaporean HDB households receive regular S&CC Rebates under government support packages (such as the Assurance Package / GST Voucher scheme).
- Rebates are automatically credited into the resident's Town Council S&CC account to offset monthly charges without requiring any manual claim.
